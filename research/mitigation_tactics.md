# Agricultural Drought Mitigation Tactics

Actionable recommendations for farmers across different drought risk levels.

## 🟡 Level: Medium Risk
*   **Mulching:** Cover soil with crop residues or plastic to reduce evaporation.
*   **Weed Control:** Remove weeds early, as they compete with crops for limited water.
*   **Alternate Wetting and Drying (AWD):** For rice, allow the field to dry slightly before reflooding.

## 🔴 Level: High Risk
*   **Life-Saving Irrigation:** If water is limited, prioritize the "Critical Stages" of the crop (e.g., flowering).
*   **Drought-Tolerant Varieties:** Prepare to switch to short-duration or drought-resistant seeds for the next cycle.
*   **Soil Moisture Monitoring:** Use tensiometers or informal "feel" tests to schedule irrigation only when absolutely needed.

## ⚫ Level: Critical Risk
*   **Thinning:** Reduce the plant population density to ensure remaining plants have enough water.
*   **Emergency Harvest:** If the crop is near maturity, harvest early as fodder to salvage some economic value.
*   **Contingency Planning:** Shift focus from yield maximization to survival and soil preservation.
