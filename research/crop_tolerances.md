# Crop Water Requirements & Drought Tolerance

This guide provides threshold data for major crops to help AI agents determine the severity of water deficits.

## 1. Maize (Corn)
*   **Critical Growth Stage:** Tasseling and Silking. Water stress here can cause 50%+ yield loss.
*   **Total Water Need:** 500 - 800 mm per season.
*   **Soil Moisture Threshold:** Avoid dropping below 50% Available Water Capacity (AWC).

## 2. Wheat
*   **Critical Growth Stage:** Flowering (Anthesis) and Grain Filling.
*   **Total Water Need:** 450 - 650 mm per season.
*   **Drought Sign:** Leaf rolling and premature yellowing of lower leaves.

## 3. Rice (Lowland)
*   **Critical Growth Stage:** Panicle initiation and Flowering.
*   **Total Water Need:** 900 - 1500 mm.
*   **Drought Tolerance:** Very low. Even short periods of soil drying below saturation can impact yield.

## 4. Sorghum / Millet
*   **Tolerance:** High. These are "Drought-Resistant" crops.
*   **Total Water Need:** 350 - 550 mm.
*   **Strategy:** Best suited for regions with high temperature and low, erratic rainfall.
