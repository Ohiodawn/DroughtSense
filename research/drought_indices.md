# Drought Risk Indices

## Standardized Precipitation Index (SPI)
The SPI is a widely used index to characterize meteorological drought on a range of timescales. It quantifies the precipitation deficit for a given period. Negative values indicate drought, while positive values indicate wet periods.

## Standardized Precipitation Evapotranspiration Index (SPEI)
The SPEI is an extension of the SPI that includes the effects of temperature on drought development through its impact on evapotranspiration. It is particularly useful for analyzing drought in the context of global warming.

## Palmer Drought Severity Index (PDSI)
The PDSI uses temperature and rainfall information in a physical water-balance model to determine relative dryness. It is effective in determining long-term drought.

## Soil Moisture Anomaly
Soil moisture is a critical factor for agricultural drought. Anomalies in soil moisture directly impact crop health and yield.
